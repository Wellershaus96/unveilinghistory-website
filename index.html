Our mission

Making history easier to discover.

History is filled with stories, people and events that continue to shape the world around us. Unveiling History develops educational content designed to make those stories accessible across modern digital formats.

What we create

One subject. Multiple ways to explore it.

01

Books

Educational history books created for readers who want to explore important events, civilizations, people and ideas in greater depth.

02

Audiobooks

History adapted into an accessible listening experience, allowing stories from the past to be explored wherever you are.

03

Short-form video

Concise educational history content designed for modern digital platforms and short-form viewing.

The approach

From historical research to digital storytelling.

01 — RESEARCH

Explore

Identify historical subjects and gather relevant source material.

02 — CREATE

Develop

Turn research into structured educational stories and content.

03 — PRODUCE

Produce

Transform stories into books, audiobooks and digital media.

04 — PUBLISH

Share

Publish content across digital platforms so more people can discover history.
